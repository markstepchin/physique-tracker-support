Answers to what you're probably thinking.

Isn't this just an iPhone album?
Albums don't align poses, date check-ins, or let you compare any two photos side by side with weight context. Recomp is built around the compare view.
How do I make the compares honest?
Same pose, same lighting, same time of day. Recomp's alignment grid helps you match framing week to week.
Do I have to track weight, workouts, or macros?
No. Photos are the core. Weight and notes are optional supporting context.
How much does it cost?
Free to start. Paid tier details at launch — honest pricing in the App Store listing.
Are my photos private?
Yes. No feed, no sharing, nothing public. Your photos stay on your device unless you choose to export.
I don't have years of photos. Does this still work?
Start tonight. The timeline compounds — meaningful compares in 3–6 weeks of consistent check-ins.

If you already take progress photos, Recomp organizes what you have. It's not a workout log or macro tracker.
