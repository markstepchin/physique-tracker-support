# 1. Deep Research Prompt

**Phase:** [1 — Deep Research & Strategy](../README.md)  
**Purpose:** Create a comprehensive guide for landing page best practices tailored to your specific product and audience.

Replace the bracketed placeholders before running the prompt.

```
I want you to take on the role of a senior UX strategist and conversion rate optimization expert who specializes in consumer App Store landing pages. You have 15+ years of experience creating high-converting landing pages that drive installs. You've studied the work of conversion experts like Joanna Wiebe, Oli Gardner, and Peep Laja, and have extensive experience optimizing pages for indie tools like Moody, CalAI, Hevy, StrongLifts, and Cluely.

OBJECTIVE:

Create a comprehensive guide for building a high-converting landing page for Recomp, a simple physique tracker for capturing progress photos and comparing check-ins side-by-side. This landing page will target athletes, bodybuilders, and anyone committed to improving their physique who need to see visual proof of change over time. The primary conversion goal is download on the App Store.

Style reference (primary): https://moody.mjarosz.com/ — polished, power-user, graph-paper feel; seamless product demo in the header; clear 3-step setup. Also study https://www.calai.app/, https://www.lifelogdoodles.com/, https://thebrainrotapp.com/, https://cluely.com/, https://shotbase.com/, StrongLifts, and Hevy for no-fluff fitness/tool pages.

DELIVERABLE REQUIREMENTS:

1. Executive summary of the most crucial landing page principles
2. Detailed breakdown of each key section with specific guidance
3. Copywriting frameworks and formulas for each section
4. Visual hierarchy and design principles
5. Mobile-first and responsive design best practices
6. CTA optimization techniques
7. Social proof and credibility elements
8. App Store download CTA design (no lead forms)
9. A/B testing recommendations
10. Landing page wireframe template with annotated sections

STRUCTURE:

1. Executive Summary
2. Core Landing Page Principles for Consumer Apps
3. Section-by-Section Breakdown
4. Hero Section
5. Problem/Agitation Section
6. Solution/Benefits Section
7. Social Proof Section
8. Feature/Benefit Section
9. Objection Handling
10. Final CTA Section
11. Copywriting Frameworks and Formulas
12. Visual Hierarchy and Design
13. Mobile Optimization
14. Conversion Rate Optimization
15. Landing Page Wireframe Template

IMPORTANT GUIDELINES:

- Focus on conversion psychology that drives App Store installs from motivated fitness users
- Provide specific examples and templates, not just general advice
- Include tactical guidance that can be implemented immediately
- Cite specific best practices from conversion experts like Joanna Wiebe, Oli Gardner, and Peep Laja
- Make recommendations specific to our industry and target audience
- Treat compare view as the product: users do not care about logs, they care about proof
- Incorporate the latest best practices from 2025, not outdated tactics

Please create a comprehensive, detailed guide that covers all these elements and serves as a complete reference for building a high-converting landing page. Include specific examples and templates where relevant.
```
